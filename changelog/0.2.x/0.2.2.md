# 0.2.2 — 2026-07-25

## Changed

- **`topics`** on `smithsonian_get_object` — indexed and freetext topic terms are deduped, first-seen order preserved; previously the two upstream blocks were concatenated raw and repeated identical terms verbatim (`siris_sil_1161076`: 10 entries, 7 distinct). ([#35](https://github.com/cyanheads/smithsonian-mcp-server/issues/35))
- **`is_cc0` / `cc0_only`** descriptions across `smithsonian_search_objects` (`ObjectSummarySchema.is_cc0`, `filters.cc0_only`), `smithsonian_get_object`, `smithsonian_get_media` (object-level and per-image `is_cc0`), `smithsonian_browse_category`, and `smithsonian_find_related` — `is_cc0` is documented as the object-level metadata license, which is CC0 throughout the Open Access corpus and so rarely varies; it cannot gate an image download. `filters.cc0_only` is documented as selecting on CC0-media presence (Lucene `media_usage:CC0`), not license status — of 14.5M records, 5.25M carry the term and the 9.24M excluded are CC0 too, just undigitized. `has_media` is named as the field that predicts a `smithsonian_get_media` outcome. ([#40](https://github.com/cyanheads/smithsonian-mcp-server/issues/40))

## Fixed

- **`smithsonian_get_media`** — added a `no_images` error reason for objects whose digitized media is entirely non-image (scanned books, 3D models, sound recordings); previously fell through both the `no_media` and `not_cc0` guards and returned a silent `images: []`. The recovery hint names the media types present at the throw site and points to `smithsonian_get_object`'s `media_summary` and `record_link`. ([#34](https://github.com/cyanheads/smithsonian-mcp-server/issues/34))
